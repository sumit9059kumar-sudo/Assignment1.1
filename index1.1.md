The Explanatory Paragraph
When you type a URL into your web browser (acting as the client), it sends an HTTP/HTTPS request across the internet to a remote server, initiating a seamless request-response cycle where the server locates the files—either pulling pre-made content from a static site or querying a database to build a dynamic web page on the fly—and sends those files back to your browser so it can render and display the website on your screen.

Part 2: How to Get Your Screenshot (Chrome DevTools)
To complete your assignment submission with the required screenshot of the Network tab, follow these quick steps on your computer:

Open Google Chrome and go to any website (e.g., google.com or github.com).

Open Developer Tools by pressing F12 on Windows/Linux or Cmd + Option + I on Mac (or right-click anywhere on the page and select Inspect).

Click on the Network tab at the top of the DevTools panel.

Refresh the webpage (F5 or Cmd + R) to capture the loading requests.
